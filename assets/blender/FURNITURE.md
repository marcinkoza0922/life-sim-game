# Walnut & sage collection

Five catalog assets, generated by `make_objects.py` and embedded automatically by the game:

| ID | Footprint | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sideboard` | 2 × 1 | Sage doors, brass pulls, turned feet, ceramic vase and books |
| `coffee_table` | 2 × 1 | Rounded walnut top, sage shelf, books and tea on a brass coaster |
| `shoe_bench` | 2 × 1 | Two upholstered seats and open shoe cubbies; seats two peons |
| `coat_rack` | 1 × 1 | Brass hooks, turned finial, hanging scarf and weighted base |
| `console_radio` | 1 × 1 | Slatted speaker grille, tuning display, brass knobs; listening interaction |

Regenerate from the repository root:

```sh
blender -b --factory-startup -P assets/blender/make_objects.py -- sideboard coffee_table shoe_bench coat_rack console_radio
```

All meshes use the existing game-space convention (front +z, y up), stay inside their
catalog footprints, and use the existing wood/fabric textures with colored PBR materials.
The RON parts provide picking geometry and fallback visuals. The radio's listening
interaction restores Fun; it does not add an audio loop.
